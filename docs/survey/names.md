## How the family names are built

| Name | Built from | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| SurView | **Sur**(face) + View | clipped subject + what you do (see) |
| TenForge | **Ten**(sile) + Forge | clipped subject + where it is made (the forge) |
| PatternFab | Pattern + **Fab**(rication) | subject + clipped craft |
| PlotTracer, PaperTrail | Plot + Tracer, Paper + Trail | subject + action |

The rule: **a short subject word (often clipped to one syllable) plus a
short craft or action word**, CamelCase, two or three syllables in all,
said in one breath. The best ones carry a second meaning (TenForge: a
forge of tensile tests and of trust).

## And TenForge?

TenForge already follows the family rule exactly (clipped subject plus
craft word), so a rename would be for a new family theme, not to fit.
What a rename would cost, measured: a public repository with three tags
and draft releases, a results and record format named
`tenforge-results` in files already written, a trademark check done in
TMview (2026-11-12), and credits in other projects' policy rows. My
advice: keep TenForge, and let the new app rhyme with it rather than
the other way round. Two ways to rhyme:

- **The "-verk" family**: Spanverk, Sundverk, Smidverk sit with TenForge
  as Swedish-rooted siblings (forge and verk are both workshops).
- **The "-Forge" pair**: FloForge (or similar) and TenForge as a matched
  test-and-simulate pair; the Forge names checked so far are crowded or
  held.

If a family theme is wanted anyway, the low-cost route is a line, not a
rename: "TenForge, SpanWorks, SurView: open tools from Katalyst Nord".
